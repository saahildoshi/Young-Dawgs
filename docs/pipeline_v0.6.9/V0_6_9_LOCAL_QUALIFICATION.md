# v0.6.9 local qualification

No local fixture passes the complete parent-matching gate. The first exact-element fixture has 4,406 nodes and 1,982 original TRI6 elements. It reproduces all 72 measured displacement histories and passes tensor-field matching at 69 states; states 35–37 (approximately 1.16591–1.18258%) fail the fixed 10% stress/elastic-strain RMS limit. The expanded fixture has 8,278 nodes and 3,736 original elements. It passes 70 states but still fails states 35–36. At state35 its SYY RMS relative error is about 82.4%, compared with 87.0% in the smaller fixture. Close displacement matching does not establish stress matching.

Both native local solves converged through all 72 prescribed histories, including histories beyond the historical crop's accepted bound. This is diagnostic convergence, not accepted crop compression. No candidate correction was selected or promoted. Boundary-force, gap/normal, continuous overlap and full path qualification remain incomplete because the prerequisite field gate already fails. Thresholds were not changed. The one registered domain expansion did not remove the onset mismatch.

Native parent and local tensor exports, local-source element maps, measured histories and fixed comparison records are retained under experiments/pipeline_v0.6.9/run_001. The original curves remain inherited accepted data. A further study must determine whether transient parent local disequilibrium, omitted neighboring contact context or incremental contact history causes these onset-only differences; the present evidence does not establish that cause.

The first per-node readback was manually stopped for efficiency and replaced with a native vector readback. An element-component entity error in the first local patch-force extraction was subsequently corrected for the expanded readback; those first patch-force values are not used as mechanical proof. The displacement/stress/strain exports and their comparison do not use that component. All unsuccessful attempts remain preserved.
