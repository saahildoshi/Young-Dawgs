# v0.6.9 individual-element contact onset reconstruction

The historical crop fails the v0.6.8 geometry/contact association gate first at 1.2675%. This is a saved converged state, not a recorded failed Newton trial. The independently exported individual contact elements, exact source-node identities and deformed quadratic curves allow a more precise description than the pair-level counts.

At 1.2575%, original source skins **95927** (nodes 6672,260,6717) and **95928** (260,6718,6720) both have native status 2. Their common original corner is node 260. The maximum stored nodal pressure is 18.0354519 MPa. Source 95927 is the nearly vertical side and 95928 the nearly horizontal adjoining side. Native target references identify **95916**, the opposing near-horizontal target. At 1.2675%, 95927 changes to status 1 and has zero stored pressure/force, while 95928 remains status 2 and its maximum pressure increases to **39.4106369 MPa**. At 1.2775%, that pressure is 43.0076942 MPa. This is redistribution at the corner, not complete loss of force transmission across the whole patch: the independent v0.6.8 opposing structural-patch-force scan still passes.

The intended physical load path is compression between these opposing exterior arcs. Remaining general contact has zero closed skins at these snapshots, so no other GC interface is shown replacing the selected pair. Exact deformed boundary identities remain unchanged. Analytic TRI6 mapping checks in v0.6.8 report no inverted element at these states. The midpoint geometric normal rotates only 0.0864 degrees for 95927, 0.1115 degrees for 95928 and 0.0379 degrees for 95916 between the two states. There is no large normal rotation supporting a simple normal-flip explanation.

The source corner at 1.2575% is (2.5621818674,2.2302372777) mm and the right target endpoint is (2.5623014445,2.2302342859) mm. At 1.2675% they are (2.5623248804,2.2300581539) and (2.5624405545,2.2300541910) mm. Thus the event occurs around a sharp adjoining-face corner with nearby target support; target deletion or a missing target inventory is not established. The quadratic midside positions and curve-normal records are retained for explicit local replay, rather than attributing the event to a midside-node defect without evidence.

The saved boundary screen reports crossings involving the vertical source skin even though its native status becomes open. Native convergence therefore does not prove physical nonpenetration. Conversely, an individual open face next to a loaded shared corner does not by itself prove complete loss of the interface load path. Both the spatial support of the pressure and continuous signed geometric overlap need independent evaluation. The existing 5e-5 mm penetration gate is unchanged; stored endpoint values are not a continuous-curve penetration bound. Status-zero fields initialized to zero by the historical exporter are not invented measurements of far-field pressure or gap.

Historical per-iteration contact telemetry contains aggregate pair information and cannot resolve the exact individual-element state during every failed trial. No per-element trial transition is inferred from those aggregate counts. A new exact-element local replay is instrumented with measured cut histories and native full tensor stress/elastic-strain output. Parent matching is required before this fixture can justify a correction or a full crop continuation. The original full specimen's earlier stress mismatch is a separate Track A problem.
