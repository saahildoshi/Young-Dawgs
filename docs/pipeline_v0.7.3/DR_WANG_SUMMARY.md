# Research summary for Dr. Wang

We established a morphology-constrained development study for P3, building on genuine microscopy-based LLM generation in v0.7.0 and verified native stress–strain evaluation in v0.7.1. The v0.7.2 revisions roughly doubled apparent stiffness by forming segmented vertical lanes; measured baseline feedback did not outperform the equal-attempt control.

The new common generator is an explicitly researcher-adapted version of captured B02. Every new calibration/development source preserves exactly 20,731 reinforcement pixels. Independently checked shape-tail, orientation and spatial-organization measures distinguish all 40 known historical lanes while accepting the reference and 12 calibration realizations. One of six additional baseline development fields fails the near-spanning rule and remains unsolved.

Seventeen feasible geometries received actual five-point ANSYS curves with independent readback. Four controlled morphology probes produced family means from 1636.45 to 1753.01 MPa, compared with baseline 1691.64 MPa and sample SD 34.92 MPa across five feasible outputs. All five predetermined H2 cases passed; observed modulus changes were 0.547–0.624%.

The registered rule proposes 1658.524267 MPa toward V3_HORIZONTAL, but rejects it: baseline separation 33.117710 MPa is below the required 69.831672 MPa. The ±2.0% proposal band contains the baseline mean. No actual target or J_target was frozen; their machine-readable values are null.

We therefore stopped before the planned model revisions and held-out evaluation, as registered. This is a qualified development-feasibility result, not a completed LLM inverse-design comparison. It neither establishes nor refutes an informative-feedback advantage. The next separately registered study should establish a more reliable constrained generator and paired geometry–mechanics probes on enough seeds to distinguish family effects from placement noise, then select a demonstrably nontrivial target. Do not relax this envelope, replace failed seeds or change this target rule after outcomes. This limited matrix does not prove that the entire reference-like design space lacks useful mechanical variability.
