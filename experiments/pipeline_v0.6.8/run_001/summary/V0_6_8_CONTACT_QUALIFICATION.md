This outcome cannot establish a recovered original full-specimen contact law.

## Selected specimen (Track B)

The 2.56 mm selected crop first used native unified general contact on all 15,408 physical faces, with independent rigid frictionless platen pairs. The licensed small C-cell verified unified contact activation and positive pressure. The original selected-crop branch reached an independently qualified 1.1% endpoint; smaller adaptive steps increased the accepted endpoint to 1.15%. At 1.2%, the solver converged, but exact deformed quadratic boundary screening found two exterior-arc crossings without a pressure-linked native general-contact target. That state and the earlier 1.175% crossing state were **rejected**, despite native convergence.

The predeclared targeted alternative removes 22 *specific native* GC skins and overlays a deformable 12-face CONTA172 source and 10-face TARGE169 target pair on the two crossed exterior arcs. A small connected compliant pair fixture closed under load, had positive pressure, 1.41×10⁻⁶ mm maximum endpoint penetration, 1.24×10⁻⁷ relative platen reaction imbalance and 5.51×10⁻⁷ relative two-sided structural-force imbalance. The first specimen setup was rejected before solving because it assumed native GC element IDs followed source boundary-array order. The corrected setup resolves native element IDs by exact oriented source face, verifies every original face exactly once, and preserves 15,386 other GC skins. Native readback confirms the installed target/contact types, real constants, and frictionless formulation.

At 0.5%, direct miscellaneous-field queries on *open* pair elements returned stale-looking pressure values. A separate licensed element-table readback found all 12 pair skins open and zero pressure. The corrected independent extractor first reads native CNSTAT, then queries contact pressure, gap and force only for active skins. The false 0.5% pressure claim and its failed force proof remain preserved as rejected diagnostic evidence.

At the accepted **1.2%** pair-branch state, the exact exterior-arc crossing is localized to source edges 2138/2139 against target edge 2637. Two native source skins are closed with positive pressure (maximum 10.462 MPa) and maximum endpoint penetration 5.601×10⁻⁷ mm, below the predeclared 5×10⁻⁵ mm limit. The native two-sided structural-force readback gives 3.80×10⁻⁴ relative opposition, below 1%. Independent upper/lower platen reactions are −3.618425 N and +3.618428 N, with 8.34×10⁻⁷ relative imbalance; both platen contact pairs pass their independent pressure, penetration and force checks. All 79,678 deformed TRI6 mappings remain positive, and the remaining GC skins are open at this checkpoint. No pore closure is claimed from this exterior-arc contact.

The contact qualification applies to each accepted Track B state separately. Later native convergence, closed skin status or positive pressure alone is not enough: any engaged interface needs native structural-force opposition, penetration, exact quadratic geometry and complete POST1 field provenance. The established Track B bound does not change the original full-specimen 10⁻⁵ mm penetration requirement.

A separately registered technical correction changed only the selected pair's CONTA172 KEYOPT(4) from nodal (2) to unified (5), retaining the same specimen, mesh, face scope, material, pressure law and acceptance gates. The licensed connected fixture and installed native model passed. A fresh zero-load branch matched the nodal branch before contact to machine-scale displacement and stress precision. The unified-pair branch has independently accepted full-stage endpoints through **1.2%**, with two closed source skins, maximum pressure 7.545 MPa, endpoint penetration 4.039×10⁻⁷ mm, and 2.63×10⁻⁸ relative opposing structural-force error. ANSYS converged through 1.5%; independent all-state POST1 and two-sided force scans establish a continuously accepted path only through load step 6/substep 7 at **1.2575%**. At the next saved state, 1.2675%, one pair skin opens while the corresponding selected exterior arcs cross. The crossing remains unqualified through 1.5%, regardless of later convergence or force balance. The last complete qualified restart stage is 1.2%; the 1.2575% field is a validated saved result inside the failed continuation, not a separate restart.
