# v0.6.7 local fixture

The fixture preserves 5527 original nodes, 2499 original TRI6 elements and 498 original physical faces exactly. It is an extracted submodel, not a remesh or a large-strain specimen demonstration. Original centroids lie in x1.70–2.24 mm, y9.95–10.24 mm. All six nodes and original connectivity are retained. Original-ID maps and the full local mesh are bound by SOURCE_EXTRACTION.json; raw arrays remain outside Git. This region contains external/top faces and no pore faces; it diagnoses the observed external-corner interaction.

Twenty-nine artificial cut faces are excluded from contact because they represent removed continuum, not physical free surfaces. Sixty cut nodes receive both measured displacement components from 15 accepted nodal-branch fields. The top rigid platen remains at its original coordinates with UY=−10.24×loading parameter. E1000 MPa, nu0.3, t1 mm, plane stress and NLGEOM are unchanged. Beyond the parent, cut motion follows the secant of the last two measured fields; this is a diagnostic hypothesis.

The first three setup trials are invalid comparisons: one omitted the second automatically generated contact type; two retained all physical faces but split the original body's section identity. Actual source readback proves the relevant original skins use type 4/section 4. Corrected trials explicitly retain that identity on all 498 physical skins; native inventories verify it. No source mesh or full restart was changed.

At the final measured parent, corrected FKN 100 core displacement RMS relative error is 5.33421917e-07; core SYY/SEQV/EYY/EXX RMS errors are [0.0010524182144294586, 0.0009428252465791797, 0.0007611921259189486, 0.001263446796219233]. Prescribed cut errors are 9.71445147e-17 mm. These pass the registered field gates. Early low-load stress matching fails: the first state's relative errors are [0.8823357358610663, 0.7983002796093269, 0.8409534891475127, 0.675956316525678]. This is reported, not removed from the history.

The nanogap/contact context is not identical: the full accepted parent has open GC, while the corrected local equilibrium develops tiny contact penetration 4.333176e-10 mm. Agreement of bulk displacement and stress does not establish identical near-touch geometry. The cut boundaries remove global compliance and shift the observed failure threshold. The fixture reproduces the qualitative onset mechanism, not the exact full-model failed displacement field.
