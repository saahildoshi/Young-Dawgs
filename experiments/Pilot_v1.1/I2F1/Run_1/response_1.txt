Revision 1 incorporates the evaluation feedback while preserving the exact solid fraction, dominant-component fraction, 4-connectivity, bidirectional percolation, dimensions, filenames, dependencies, phase convention, and deterministic seed behavior.

The principal changes are:

* **Lineal-path statistics are now explicitly optimized**, rather than being controlled only indirectly through morphology.
* Candidate selection is now **two-stage**:

  1. 32 procedural candidates per seed are evaluated for thickness, pore size, connectivity, and \(S_2\).
  2. The best 6 candidates are additionally evaluated against the supplied lineal-path curve.
* **\(S_2\) receives stronger selection pressure**, especially at the intermediate and long radii responsible for most remaining spatial-statistics error.
* Scalar descriptor tolerances were tightened to prevent gains in \(S_2\)/lineal path from sacrificing the already-close thickness and pore metrics.
* The lineal-path calculation uses a **deterministic 32×32 origin grid and eight orientations**, making it practical for candidate ranking without changing output stochasticity.
* Every candidate is still rejected unless it has the exact solid-pixel budget, exact dominant-component budget, and both LR/TB percolation.
