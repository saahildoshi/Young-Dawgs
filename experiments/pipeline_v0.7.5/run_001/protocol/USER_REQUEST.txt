**Does providing measured mechanical response data to an LLM improve its ability to revise a stochastic microstructure generator toward a specified apparent elastic modulus, compared with an otherwise matched revision that receives no measured mechanical feedback?**

1. Improvement from a second opportunity to revise the generator.

6. Numerical differences caused by finite-element discretization.
7. Structural failures and morphology-family violations.
